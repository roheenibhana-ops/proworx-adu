WHAT IS AN ADU?

A Second Home, Right On Your Property

An Accessory Dwelling Unit is a self-contained living space. A converted basement, a garage conversion, or a standalone backyard cottage. Built on a single-family lot. It's one of the fastest ways to add rental income, house family, or increase your property's value in Utah.

Detached, attached and basement/garage-conversion ADUs
Full permitting handled for Salt Lake, Utah, Davis & Summit counties
Fixed-price plan tiers so there are no surprises on cost
Built by the same licensed & insured team behind Pro-Worx Construction

CHOOSE YOUR PATH

Attached or Detached. Which Fits Your Property?

Both paths are fully permitted, code-compliant and finished to the same standard. The right one comes down to your lot, budget and timeline.

Attached ADU

The faster, more affordable path. We convert space you already have.

Uses your existing basement or garage footprint
Shorter timeline. Usually 3–5 weeks
Lower cost since foundation, framing and roof already exist
Ideal for family living or a straightforward rental

Detached ADU

A standalone backyard structure. Maximum privacy and flexibility.

Fully separate structure with its own entrance
Now easier to build statewide under Utah's SB284 law. See our 2026 ADU law page
Best privacy for renters or extended family
Higher cost, but the most flexible long-term asset

Have Questions About Building an ADU?

Most single-family lots in Salt Lake, Utah, Davis and Summit counties qualify, but rules vary by city. We handle a full zoning and permit review as part of every free estimate, so you'll know exactly what's possible before committing to anything.

Typical ADU investment in Utah runs $100K–$200K for a garage or basement conversion, and $200K–$300K for a full detached build. Final pricing depends on your lot, utility access and finish level. We'll give you an exact number after a site walk.

Garage and basement conversions typically take 3–5 weeks. Detached, ground-up ADUs run longer once permitting and foundation work are factored in. We'll give you a realistic timeline before you sign anything.

ADUs are one of the highest-return additions available to Utah homeowners, both through added square footage and rental income potential. Many owners recoup their investment through rent within a few years.
