Do I Need a Permit for an ADU in Draper?

Most ADU permits in Salt Lake County are processed through the Draper building and planning department. We handle intake, plan review coordination and inspection scheduling for you from start to finish, so you never have to deal with the city directly.

What's different about ADUs in Draper. Draper's hillside neighborhoods around Suncrest and Corner Canyon bring steep-slope lots into play, which usually means more engineering for grading and drainage than a flat-lot build elsewhere in the valley.

What Does Utah's New ADU Law (SB284) Mean for Draper Homeowners?

Utah's SB284 now requires most cities to allow one detached ADU per qualifying lot statewide. Draper's HOA-governed hillside communities often have stricter architectural review than city code alone. Plan for both approvals, not just one. See the full breakdown on our 2026 ADU law page →.

From Suncrest's Slopes to The Point's Mega-Development

Draper spans both Salt Lake and Utah counties and covers some of the most varied terrain of any city we build in:

Suncrest

A hillside master-planned community that actually straddles the Draper/Highland line, with steep-slope lots and mountain views.

Corner Canyon

The area around Corner Canyon High School, part of Draper's east-bench foothill terrain.

The Point

The former Utah State Prison site, now a massive mixed-use redevelopment reported in the billions of dollars — a distinctly Draper-specific landmark project reshaping the city's north end.

Because Draper genuinely runs from flat valley-floor lots to steep hillside parcels within the same city limits, we never quote an ADU here without walking the specific property first.

What Draper's Ordinance Actually Requires

Draper City Code §9-5-210 covers two ADU types — detached (D-ADU) and internal (I-ADU) — each with its own standards:

RequirementDraper Standard
Minimum lot size, detached ADU12,000 sq ft
Minimum lot size, internal ADU6,000 sq ft, fronting a public street
Max ADU size50% of the primary home's square footage
Height limit, detached ADU35 ft, matching the primary home's materials and color
Parking1 additional on-site space, no tandem or blocked spaces
Lease termMinimum 30 days — no short-term rentals

Owner-occupancy is required and re-verified annually at renewal, only one ADU is allowed per lot, and both ADU types share utility meters with the main house rather than getting a separate connection. We confirm the exact permit fee and your specific zone with Draper's Planning and Development Department before pricing your project, since that figure isn't published in the general code.

Draper's Code Doesn't Single Out Hillside Lots — But Your Site Work Will

Unlike Alpine, Draper's ordinance doesn't carry a distinct hillside or sensitive-lands review specifically for ADUs. That doesn't mean the terrain stops mattering: a Suncrest or Corner Canyon lot on real slope still needs the same grading, drainage and foundation engineering any steep-lot build requires, it just isn't triggered by an ADU-specific code section. We scope that engineering the same way we would for any hillside project in Draper, separate from the standard permit review.
