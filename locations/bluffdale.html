Bluffdale Still Feels Rural in Places — And Newer in Others

Salt Lake County's southernmost city has held onto a semi-rural, equestrian character even as growth pushes north from Utah County and south from Draper. That mix shows up block by block:

Outside these newer communities, much of Bluffdale still carries larger, older agricultural-heritage lots — part of why the city's ADU ordinance is written to work across both without carving out separate rules by zone.

How Bluffdale Actually Sizes an ADU

Bluffdale City Code Chapter 11.340 allows both internal (I-ADU) and detached (D-ADU) accessory dwelling units in residential, mixed-use and special-district zones, with the size cap tied directly to your existing home:

RequirementBluffdale Standard
Minimum lot size6,000 sq ft
Max ADU size50% of the primary home's gross square footage
Parking, detached ADU2 additional off-street spaces
Parking, internal ADU1 additional off-street space
Units per propertyOne ADU maximum

Owner-occupancy is required in either the main home or the ADU, a detached unit needs a permanent foundation (no trailers or manufactured units used as a workaround), and Bluffdale doesn't allow a separate utility meter or street address for the ADU. Bluffdale's 6,000-sq-ft minimum is already more permissive than the 11,000-sq-ft floor set by Utah's SB284, effective October 1, 2026, but we confirm the city's current compliance status with Community Development before pricing your project.

Thinking Airbnb? Bluffdale's Ordinance Says No.

Bluffdale requires a minimum 30-day occupancy for an ADU, which rules out nightly or short-term rental platforms entirely. If your goal is extended family housing or a genuine long-term tenant, that's not a problem — but if you were planning a short-term rental income stream, Bluffdale's ordinance is built specifically to prevent that use.
